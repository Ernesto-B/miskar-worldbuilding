WHEN PARTY STALLS

1. Summarize options.
2. Ask: what info are you missing?
3. Ask: what is your next action?
4. Offer 3 obvious paths.
5. Remind: time may pass.
6. Lock decision with caller/vote.
7. Remind party: “You do not need to solve the whole situation right now. What is your next concrete action?”
