## Design Checklist
- [ ] Difficulty from situation complexity, not capability degradation
- [ ] No prone from environmental effects (positional threat with warning instead)
- [ ] No enemy with AC 18+ as the sole difficulty mechanic
- [ ] Every player has something meaningful to do on every turn
- [ ] Key mechanic is discoverable within round 1
- [ ] At least one non-violent solution path exists
